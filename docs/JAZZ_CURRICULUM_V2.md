# Jazz Academy V2: Learning Path Roadmap

The Jazz Academy is being restructured into three core pillars (Learning Paths) to provide a guided journey instead of a scattered collection of lessons.

## Pillar 1: The Swing Foundations
*Focus: Internalizing the jazz pulse and basic vocabulary.*
1. **The Ghost Note:** Introduction to jazz articulation and tongue blocking.
2. **Swing 8ths:** Understanding the "Triplet" feel vs straight 8ths.
3. **The Jazz Accent:** Emphasizing off-beats (2 & 4) to create syncopation.

## Pillar 2: The Blues Lab
*Focus: The roots of improvisation using the Blues scale.*
1. **The 12-Bar Grid:** Understanding the standard blues structure.
2. **The Blue Note:** Mastering the flat-5th (Gb in C Blues) and its resolution.
3. **Call & Response:** Building a conversation with the saxophone using short riffs.

## Pillar 3: Melodic Improvisation
*Focus: Connecting chords and building real solos.*
1. **The ii-V-I Bridge:** Navigating the most important chord progression in jazz.
2. **Guide Tones:** Using 3rds and 7ths to outline harmony effortlessly.
3. **The Motif Builder:** How to take a simple idea and develop it into a solo.

---
**Technical Note:** All paths now support automatic transposition for Alto and Tenor saxophones.
